Welcome to Residence Life 101!

This website is designed to help Resident Advisors (RAs) at James Madison University navigate the requirements of the job and offer helpful links to make their lives a bit easier! Throughout the site, we'll discuss how to order bulletin boards and where to locate the options to choose from, how to write a good community report, how to navigate the StarRez software, and how to get in touch with your fellow RAs.
